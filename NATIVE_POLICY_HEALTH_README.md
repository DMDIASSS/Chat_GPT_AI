Which training or replay-label stage originally caused that preference is not established by the supplied logs.

The overnight logs show genuine optimizer updates and successful saves, usually near-zero average reward and normalized policy entropy around 0.28–0.35. They do not establish useful mechanic mastery. Repeated kickoff imitation reduces its validation loss but does not certify playing strength. The frozen native opponents are weak copies; fast native simulation alone cannot transfer update180's attention weights into this separate MLP.

Stop running native training before extracting/building this update. Existing weights are preserved. NATIVE_2_BUILD.bat upgrades only known verified upstream source versions, handles Windows CRLF and restores console Policy Loss (actor loss), Critic Loss, Mean KL Divergence, Post-update PPO KL, Policy Entropy, Learner Speed, Learner Idle Fraction and Learner Touch Fraction. PPO samples stochastic controls; a moving training rollout does not establish that greedy exported play moves.

PPO KL measures policy change during an update. With one epoch and one batch, upstream's pre-update Mean KL can be zero; the new post-update estimate samples up to 1,024 collected actions after the optimizer step. It is an approximate PPO KL, not KL against Nexto/update180. Teacher KL and actor cross entropy in the recovery report measure fit to the teacher on validation episodes; lower is closer, not a playing-strength score. Current native output has no legacy attention-trainer teacher KL unless a teacher dataset is explicitly used.

Rebuild changes executable/library identities. Old overnight/native runs keep their checkpoints but cannot resume under the new binary; use a new run name when starting further experiments.
