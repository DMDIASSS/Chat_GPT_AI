# Research basis and exact implementation scope

The training system makes testable hypotheses. None of these papers guarantees improvement for these Rocket League actors, this reward basis or this hardware. A successful implementation test is not evidence of stronger play.

| Paper | Mechanism used here | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| [Ng, Harada, Russell: Policy invariance under reward transformations](https://wordpress.andrewng.org/index.php/publication/policy-invariance-under-reward-transformations-theory-and-application-to-reward-shaping/) | All candidate shaping terms use gamma*Phi(next)-Phi(current); true goal terminals have Phi=0. The base goal outcome stays +/-10. | Optimal-policy invariance has assumptions about the discounted task and boundary handling; it does not promise that finite neural PPO improves. |
| [Pardo et al.: Time Limits in Reinforcement Learning, ICML 2018](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/pardo18a.html) | A rollout boundary is a sampling truncation, with critic bootstrap and retained next-state potential. It does not reset the physical match. | A genuine timed-match terminal would need different treatment; this trainer optimizes first-goal episodes, while validation uses timed multi-goal matches. |
| [Schulman et al.: Generalized Advantage Estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02438) | Gamma .999, lambda .97, correctly ordered bootstrap states and stopped traces between separate player fragments. | Value estimates are approximate; low critic loss alone is not proof of skill. |
| [Jaderberg et al.: Population Based Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.09846) | A small serial population of reward/learning-rate variants, external match scores, copying retained actor initialization and bounded coefficient mutation. | This is a laptop adaptation, not the paper's asynchronous distributed implementation. Each branch has its own resumable critic/optimizer. |
| [Vinyals et al.: AlphaStar, Nature 2019](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-019-1724-z) / [author explanation](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphastar-grandmaster-level-in-starcraft-ii-using-multi-agent-reinforcement-learning/) | Frozen original opponents, accepted history, current self-play and a mixture favoring opponents with lower measured score while preserving exploration. | Not a reproduction of AlphaStar, its scale, architecture or full exploiter league. Initial native opponents are Chronos and Galactus; other names are not silently represented by substitutes. |
| [Schmitt et al.: Kickstarting Deep Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.03835) | Original pretrained actor initialization plus a tunable teacher-policy KL term to limit forgetting. | The shipped implementation uses the same-family retained actor as teacher. It does not merge heterogeneous weights or claim the paper's benchmark gains. |
| [Chen et al.: Learning by Cheating, CoRL 2020](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v100/chen20a.html) | Reviewed for training-time privileged teachers and public-only exported students. | Its result is about vision-based driving, not a universal guarantee. These actors already receive simulator-derived game features. No new privileged actor is claimed in this revision. |
| [Pinto et al.: Asymmetric Actor Critic](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.06542) | Reviewed as an optional future controlled critic ablation. | Earlier ordinary/private-critic pilots did not establish an advantage here. Private inputs are therefore not enabled by default, and no private inputs enter exports. |

## Reward generation/search

Phi is a weighted sum of five public-state terms: team-relative ball position; negative car-ball distance; team-relative ball velocity; negative own-goal threat; and proximity-weighted possession advantage. Coefficients are bounded. Control preserves the earlier position/distance basis. Attack, defense and possession supply initial alternatives. Every eight completed trials, the coordinator can replace a weak non-control population member with a bounded numerical mutation of a measured alternative. This generates coefficient combinations, not arbitrary executable reward programs. Each branch's recipe records the exact coefficients; resuming a branch does not change its reward function.

No reward is paid directly for touching, boosting, spinning, demoing or being airborne. Dense rewards are transition potential differences. Curriculum and stronger learned reward networks remain research options rather than unsupported shipped claims.

## Selection and evidence

Training reward does not rank reward recipes: different scales would bias that comparison. The coordinator uses paired candidate/baseline timed WDL and goal difference against fixed originals. Positive screens need fresh-seed confirmation before promotion. Apparent regressions are also confirmed before a branch is retired. Repeated adaptive comparisons can still produce errors; intervals are approximate Student-t summaries, not a lifetime statistical guarantee. The retained model is empirically protected, not proven unbeatable or monotonically improving against every opponent.

The new collector must account for every learner decision in its prepared PPO batch. Optimizer sample-use counters separately report actual applied updates and repeated epochs. An early KL stop can still limit optimization of a batch; do not equate prepared samples with complete optimization passes.
